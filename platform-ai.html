Case study · Enterprise platforms and AI

Platform operations and integration readiness

Enterprise collaboration tools have everyday operational needs: reliable access, clear administration and support for users. AI integrations add further questions about authorisation, data access and readiness. I worked across platform operations and the testing needed to assess those integrations.

My responsibility
Platform administration, access governance and integration coordination
Scope of work
Work management, SSO, OAuth, sandbox testing and user acceptance
Deliverables
Access workflows, test coordination and security-review preparation

The problem

An integration needed more than a successful connection

Connecting a tool did not establish whether it was appropriate for the intended users and accounts. Authorisation behaviour, access boundaries and the practical user experience also needed examination. At the same time, the existing platform still required administration and adoption support.

My contribution

Coordinate the operational and testing work

I administered the enterprise work-management platform and supported access governance, SSO lifecycle activity and user adoption. For AI integrations, I coordinated sandbox testing, OAuth configuration, security reviews and UAT. I worked with stakeholders and the vendor to clarify observed behaviour and outstanding requirements.

A delivery decision

Use controlled testing to establish readiness

I helped test whether the connection and account behaviour matched the intended configuration, raised questions when results did not match expectations and coordinated follow-up. Testing and security review informed readiness discussions; a working demonstration alone did not establish approval for broader use.

Outcome

Practical support for platform users and integration decisions

The contribution included platform administration, access workflows, integration testing and review coordination. I also presented an AI-enabled operations use case at the 2026 AI@Work Hackathon. This experience is in AI enablement and governance; it does not claim ownership of training or developing foundation models.
