02 / What good looks like

A program is healthier when the hard questions become easier to answer.

A

Who owns the next move?

Clear ownership turns a broad concern into an action that can actually be completed.

B

What evidence changes the decision?

Good reporting reduces noise; it makes risk, trade-offs and progress visible enough to act on.

C

Will this still work next quarter?

The answer should be automation, standards and a process people can use without a hero in the room.
