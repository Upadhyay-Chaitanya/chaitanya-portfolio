Case study · Security delivery

From findings to owner actions

Remediation involves more than distributing a list of findings. Owners need the affected resource details, a clear action and an understanding of the impact. My role was to coordinate that work across technical teams, security reviewers and business stakeholders.

My responsibility
Remediation coordination, evidence tracking and escalation
Scope of work
Cloud access, exposure, configuration findings and exception requests
Deliverables
Owner actions, remediation trackers, runbooks and review evidence

The problem

The available data did not always answer the next question

Security notifications, usage reports and live finding views could describe different things. A usage count did not necessarily map to an individual finding link, and a reported finding could need validation against the current resource configuration. Those differences mattered when routing work or preparing an exception request.

My contribution

Reconcile the evidence and coordinate the work

I worked through the available finding data, identified missing evidence, contacted owners and tracked their responses. I coordinated remediation across access controls and cloud configuration workstreams, including SSO migration, least-privilege reviews and the handling of recovery access. I also maintained escalation paths and status updates.

A delivery decision

Treat remediation, missing evidence and exceptions separately

Some owners could remediate; others needed to explain an ongoing business requirement. I kept those actions distinct in the coordination work and worked with security reviewers on the evidence needed for an exception. A request for more time remained a request until the appropriate review and approval were complete.

Outcome

A documented route through remediation and review

The work supported access remediation, exposure-reduction campaigns and owner-specific follow-up. Trackers and runbooks captured the required actions and outstanding dependencies. This was a coordination role: technical owners performed or validated changes, and exception decisions remained with the authorised reviewers.
